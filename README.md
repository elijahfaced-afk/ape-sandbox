# Ape Sandbox

A browser horror sandbox: you are an ape in a huge concrete facility, and killer apes hunt you. Spawn props, throw them, blow them up, and survive the blood moon.

**Play:** https://elijahfaced-afk.github.io/ape-sandbox/

Controls (PC): WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, mouse look, V first/third person, Q spawn menu, B blood moon, K killers on/off.

Phone / tablet: on-screen stick to walk, drag to look, buttons for use, jump, freeze, run, tool, E, menu and view.

VR (Quest browser or PC VR): press ENTER VR. Left stick walk, left trigger run, right stick turn, right trigger use tool, grip freeze, A jump, B next tool, X spawn, Y next prop.
